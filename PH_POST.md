Title: AI Post Composer (MVP)

Tagline: Fast template-driven post composer for LinkedIn and X — write, refine, and copy posts in seconds.

Short description:
AI Post Composer helps creators, students, and makers write better social posts faster. The MVP ships with platform-aware templates (short posts, threads, promos, personal stories), tone presets, and one-click copy-to-clipboard — no signup required.

Long description (for PH body):
Built for students and indie makers who want to share projects and stories without the friction, AI Post Composer is a tiny Chrome extension that generates platform-aware posts from curated templates and tone selectors. Install in seconds, pick a template, add context, and copy your post.

The MVP focuses on immediate value with client-side templates and no external services — perfect for quick composition and sharing. For users who want one-click purchases, the app is available as a downloadable ZIP (install unpacked) — INSTALL.md in the repo explains the steps. Pro features (LLM-powered generation, scheduling, premium templates and Chrome Store publish) are planned after validation. Launch deal: lifetime access for early adopters.
